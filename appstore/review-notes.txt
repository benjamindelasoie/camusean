CAMUSEAN — APP REVIEW NOTES
(Paste into App Store Connect → [your build] → App Review Information → Notes. Max 4000 chars.)

Camusean is a hands-free reading companion. While reading a physical book in a
foreign language, you speak an unknown word aloud and instantly hear its
definition spoken back, then review saved words as flashcards later. The voice
lookup is the core, differentiating feature — please test it directly.

No account or login is required. The dictionary service is pre-configured, so
everything works on first launch with nothing to set up.

IMPORTANT — the core feature is voice-based and must be tested on a PHYSICAL
DEVICE. iOS speech recognition does not run in the Simulator, so on a Simulator
the app will look like a simple flashcard app. On a real device it is a
voice-driven lookup tool. To experience it:

1. Open the app -> "Read" tab -> tap "Begin Reading".
2. When prompted, allow Microphone and Speech Recognition.
3. The app is now listening — no button to hold. Just say a French word aloud,
   e.g. "bibliotheque" or "flaneur".
4. Pause. Within about one second you will HEAR the English definition
   spoken aloud, see it on screen, and the word is saved automatically.
   The app keeps listening, so you can say the next word straight away.
5. Tap "End session" when done.
6. Open the "Review" tab to study saved words as flashcards
   (swipe right = learned, swipe left = repeat).

Default reading language is French. It can be changed in Settings, which also
offers English, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese.

WHY THE APP ASKS FOR THE CAMERA
You can optionally attach a book to a reading session, so saved words are grouped
by the book you met them in and definitions pick the sense that fits that book.
On the "Read" start screen, the selector above "Begin Reading" offers
"Add a book...", which lets you type the title by hand OR tap
"Scan a barcode instead".

Scanning is the ONLY use of the camera. It reads the ISBN barcode entirely
on-device via VisionKit, saves no photo or video, and transmits no image
anywhere. Only the resulting ISBN number is sent to Open Library
(openlibrary.org, the Internet Archive's free book catalogue) to look up the
title, author and cover art.

The camera is entirely optional in two senses: adding a book accepts typed
details instead, and the app's core voice lookup works fully without ever adding
a book (choose "Free reading").

WHAT THE SECOND LINE UNDER A DEFINITION IS
When you say an inflected word, the result card shows a short grammar note under
the definition, e.g. "Past participle of disparaitre". It is displayed but
deliberately never spoken aloud: the spoken definition is English-only so the
English text-to-speech voice does not mispronounce foreign words.

PRIVACY
No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Saved words and books live
only on the device and are never uploaded. There are exactly two outbound
requests, neither tied to any identity:
  1. The recognized word or phrase, your selected languages, and the book title
     if you picked one -> Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the definition.
  2. A scanned or typed ISBN -> Open Library, which returns title/author/cover.
