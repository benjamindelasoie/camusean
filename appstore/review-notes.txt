CAMUSEAN — APP REVIEW NOTES
(Paste into App Store Connect → [your build] → App Review Information → Notes. Max 4000 chars.)

Camusean is a hands-free reading companion: while reading a physical book in a
foreign language, you speak an unknown word aloud and instantly hear its
definition spoken back, then review saved words as flashcards later. The voice
lookup is the core, differentiating feature — please test it directly.

No account or login is required. Everything works on first launch (the
dictionary service is pre-configured).

IMPORTANT — the core feature is voice-based and must be tested on a PHYSICAL
DEVICE. iOS speech recognition does not run in the Simulator, so on a Simulator
the app will look like a simple flashcard app. On a real device it is a
voice-driven lookup tool. To experience it:

1. Open the app → "Read" tab → tap "Start Session".
2. When prompted, allow Microphone and Speech Recognition.
3. Hold the push-to-talk button and say a French word aloud,
   e.g. "bibliothèque" or "flâneur".
4. Release. Within about one second you will HEAR the English definition
   spoken aloud, see it on screen, and the word is saved automatically.
5. Open the "Review" tab to study saved words as flashcards (swipe left = known).

Default reading language is French; it can be changed in Settings.

This is a single, original app built by one independent developer — not a
template, reskin, or clone. The concept (speak-a-word-aloud-while-reading) and
all code are original. We are happy to answer any questions or schedule a call
to walk through the app.
