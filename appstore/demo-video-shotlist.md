# Camusean — demo video shot list

Goal: in ~25 seconds, prove the differentiating feature is real and works — a
reviewer (or a friend) watches one continuous voice lookup and immediately
"gets it." Record on a PHYSICAL DEVICE (speech recognition is dead in the
Simulator). One clean take is better than fancy editing.

Two uses:
1. Attach it in the Resolution Center reply to the 4.3 rejection.
2. Optionally upload as an App Preview (15–30s, portrait, recorded on device).

## Setup before recording
- Real iPhone, not Simulator. Quiet room.
- Hold a physical foreign-language book in frame if you can — it sells the
  use case in one glance.
- Pre-grant Microphone + Speech Recognition permission BEFORE recording, so the
  permission dialogs don't eat your 30 seconds (do one throwaway lookup first).
- Volume up — the spoken definition is the payoff; it must be audible.
- Reading language = French in Settings.
- Optional: screen-record with Settings → Control Center → Screen Recording, and
  enable the mic in the recording so the spoken definition is captured. (A second
  phone filming you holding the book + speaking is even more convincing.)

## Beats

0:00–0:03  Open on the Read tab. (Optional voice/caption: "Reading in French.")
           Book visible in hand.

0:03–0:06  Tap "Start Session."

0:06–0:12  Hold the push-to-talk button and clearly say one word:
           "flâneur"  (or "bibliothèque" — pick one that reads cleanly).
           Release the button.

0:12–0:18  PAYOFF: the English definition is spoken aloud + appears on screen.
           Let it play fully — do not cut early. This is the whole pitch.

0:18–0:22  Tap the "Review" tab. The word you just looked up is sitting there
           as a flashcard. Swipe one card.

0:22–0:25  End on the flashcard deck. (Optional caption: "Every word you say,
           saved to review.")

## Captions (if you add text overlays — keep them tiny/literary, no marketing slop)
- "Hit a word you don't know."
- "Say it out loud."
- "Hear what it means."
- "Review it later."

## Don'ts
- Don't show the Simulator.
- Don't speed-ramp past the spoken definition — its audibility is the proof.
- Don't add music that drowns the spoken definition.
