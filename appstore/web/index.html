Read in another language
without breaking your stride.

Say an unknown word aloud while you read, and hear what it means — without putting the book down or losing the sentence.

How it works

  1. Start a reading session and keep the book in your hands.
  2. Hit a word you don't know? Hold the button and say it out loud.
  3. Hear the definition spoken back in your own language, instantly.
  4. Find every word you said waiting as a flashcard, ready to review.

Built for real reading

  • Voice-first. Designed for hands-on-the-book reading, not screen tapping.
  • Spoken definitions. Your eyes stay on the page.
  • For books and flow, not drills, streaks, or gamified busywork.
  • Your languages. Read in one, hear definitions in your own.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads; your words stay on your device.
